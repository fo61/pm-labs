# Bounty Research Checklist

The checklist I run before writing any paid bounty submission. It exists because the costliest mistake in bounty work is producing good content for a listing that is already closed, pays in something other than cash, or requires something I cannot honestly do.

## 1. Listing validity

- [ ] Listing page is open and shows remaining time (not 0h:0m:0s, not In Review or Completed)
- [ ] Submission deadline is read from the page body or sponsor comment, not inferred from the winner announcement date
- [ ] Prize text confirms cash/token payment and does not substitute tickets, points, or exposure
- [ ] Current submission count is recorded, so effort can be sized against competition

## 2. Requirements

- [ ] Deliverable format is explicit (post, thread, article, video, PR) including any length floor
- [ ] Required tags, mentions, and links are listed verbatim
- [ ] Language requirement is noted
- [ ] Any wallet, account, or platform actions required (connect, follow, test a product) are listed, with whether they spend funds

## 3. Honesty screen

- [ ] Nothing in the deliverable requires inventing personal experience, usage history, or results
- [ ] No step requires spending the user's funds without a separate, explicit approval
- [ ] No step requires credentials, seed phrases, or private keys to change hands (they never do)

## 4. Source discipline

- [ ] Every number in the draft traces to a primary source (official announcement, docs, on-chain record) or is flagged as an unverified estimate
- [ ] Rounded figures are written as rounded (~300M, not 300,000,000.0)
- [ ] Anything I could not verify is either removed or explicitly marked unverified

## 5. Submission

- [ ] Final copy is shown to the account owner before posting under their identity
- [ ] Post/submission link is captured immediately after publishing
- [ ] Deadline, prize, and submission link are logged together for follow-up
